# Datasheet EC66 (Elite Robots, Datasheet EC-Series 1.1) [D]
- Arm: payload 6 kg; reach 914 mm; repeatability +-0.03 mm; 6 axes; J1-J6 +-360 deg.
- Joint speeds: J1-J2 150 deg/s, J3 190 deg/s, J4-J6 260 deg/s. Typical TCP speed 1.5 m/s.
- IP54 arm, -10..50 C, noise <70 dB, typical power 250 W, any mounting angle, footprint dia 150 mm,
  weight 17.5 kg, cable 5.5 m (1.5/3/8 m optional). ISO 10218; RoHS; UL, CE, CR, KCs.
- Tool I/O connector 12 pin: 2 DI, 2 DO, 1 AI, 1 AO; 24 V/2 A; RS485, Modbus RTU.
- Programming: "LUA-script, free-drag/offline programming".
- STANDARD controller: AC/DC; 16 DI, 20 DO (marketing text: 16 DI/16 DO), 2 AI, 4 AO; 24V/2A I/O supply;
  comms: RS485/RS232, Ethernet TCP/IP, UDP, Modbus TCP/RTU, EtherNet/IP slave, Profinet slave, CCLink slave (opt).
  505x246x447 mm, IP44, 15 kg, 90-264 VAC or 19-72 VDC.
- MINI controller (optional OEM "Mini" DC controller "for integrators, OEM partners and users who want to
  create their own custom design"): DC 45-55 V; 8 DI/8 DO; RS485, Ethernet, UDP, Modbus TCP/RTU, EIP, Profinet,
  CCLink(opt); 205x135x40 mm, IP40, 1.2 kg.  => the user's "two control boxes": standard vs Mini (OEM).
- Teach pendant ERP300: 8" resistive touch 800x600, IP54, 1.2 kg, 5.5 m cable, 3-position enable key.
